STEP 1

STEP 1: Choose Public Parameters (p & g)

Select a prime modulus (p) and a base generator (g). These parameters are shared publicly across the unsecure network.

Why do we need Prime p & Generator g?

Modulus (p): A shared prime number defining the mathematical field.
Generator (g): A base primitive root modulo p (where 1 < g < p).
Both p and g are known publicly to Alice, Bob, and any network listener.

STEP 2

STEP 2: Choose Private Secrets (a & b) & Calculate Public Keys

Alice chooses secret a and Bob chooses secret b. Both calculate their public keys to exchange.

How are Public Keys calculated?

Alice's Public Key: A = ga mod p
Bob's Public Key: B = gb mod p
Secrets a and b are never sent across the network.
